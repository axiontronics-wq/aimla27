About

About the Conference

The 5th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Applications, AIMLA 2027 (IEEE Conference Record 72383), brings together academicians, researchers and data analysts to promote the adoption of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. As organizations worldwide go through digital transformation, AI/ML applications connect analytics, data science and automation to speed that journey up.

The conference is a platform where researchers and innovators from different disciplines present their work, share knowledge and exchange ideas. It is organized by the School of Electrical Sciences, K.S.Rangasamy College of Technology.

Host

About the Institution

K.S.Rangasamy College of Technology (Autonomous) is one of the leading institutions in Tamil Nadu, India, and serves the technical education needs of rural students. It believes science and technology should solve social issues. The college was started in 1994 and received autonomous status in 2007.

  • Accredited by NAAC A++ (2023 to 2028), score 3.56
  • 8 UG programmes and 1 PG programme accredited by NBA under Tier-I
  • Recognized under Section 2(f) and 12B by UGC
  • NIRF: ranked 99 (2017); 101-150 (2018); Innovation rank band 51-100 (2023); Engineering rank band 201-300 (2024)
  • QS I-Gauge Diamond rating
  • Anna University approved research centre for 20 departments
  • IPR awards from Anna University twice
  • Research ecosystem: NTTM (Rs. 8.48+ crores), DBT-STAR College, M.Tech. Biotech sponsored by DBT, DST-FIST sponsored institution, TNSCST IPR Nodal Centre
  • Start-up ecosystem: MSME sponsored incubator, Atal Community Innovation Centre supported by AIM / NITI Aayog, AICTE IDEA Lab, StartupTN

Vision

To nurture highly competent professionals and researchers through quality education driven by innovation, technology, collaboration and societal relevance.

Mission

Academic excellence: to impart quality education in Science, Engineering, Technology and Management through outcome based, student-centric and innovative teaching-learning practices.

Faculty, students and academic ecosystem: to empower faculty and students through multidisciplinary, continuous professional development, advanced infrastructure, collaboration and life-long learning for academic excellence.

Research, innovation and societal impact: to foster research, innovation, entrepreneurship and emerging technologies for sustainable development and societal impact with professional ethics.

Publication Policy

All accepted and presented papers that comply with IEEE guidelines will be published in IEEE Xplore. AIMLA 2027 submits all presented papers to IEEE. IEEE may decline any paper that does not meet its standards, and AIMLA 2027 is not responsible for papers IEEE does not publish.
