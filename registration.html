Registration Fee

Once a paper is selected, authors have to register by submitting the Camera Ready Paper with fee payment. Payment procedures will be sent to the author's mail ID.

Mode of presentation: read this before you submit

  • Hybrid (online) mode is only for foreign authors.
  • Indian authors must present offline (in person) at K.S.Rangasamy College of Technology, Tiruchengode.
  • For Indian authors, at least one author of every paper must present offline.

Important note

  • The official website of this conference is https://aimla.ksrct.ac.in/.
  • All conference communication is sent only through Microsoft CMT mail or the official conference email, aimla@ksrct.ac.in.
  • The conference fee is accepted only by bank transfer. We never use QR codes or any other link for payment.
  • If you receive a suspicious message, link or QR code, contact us before you act on it.
